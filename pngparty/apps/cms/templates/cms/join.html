Membership is open to citizens in every province.

How to join

Online sign-up is coming soon. For now, register with a field agent.

  1. Meet a field agent in your ward or at a party event. Bring your NID card if you have one.
  2. The agent registers you on their phone and takes your photo. This works with no signal.
  3. Your provincial coordinator checks the registration and gives you a membership number.
  4. Collect your card. Its QR code lets anyone confirm you are a verified member.
